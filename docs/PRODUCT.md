# Product Overview

sabo-mutu is a software engineering company specializing in building high-quality, scalable solutions for clients across various industries. The platform focuses on professional career matching, market research integration, and intelligent learning capabilities powered by an AI learning engine.

## Core Features

1. **Career Matcher** — Matches professionals with suitable career opportunities based on skills, preferences, and market demand.
2. **Market Research** — Aggregates and analyzes industry trends, salary data, and job market insights.
3. **Learning Engine** — Personalized learning paths that adapt to user progress and goals, powered by AI recommendations.
